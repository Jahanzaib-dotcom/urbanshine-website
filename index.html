Help & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

Find transparent answers regarding our drying times, equipment, guarantees, and service scope.

How long do carpets and couches take to dry?
Thanks to our industrial-grade high-velocity moisture recovery vacuums, carpets and couches are left only lightly damp. Typical drying times range between 2 to 4 hours, depending on room ventilation and airflow.
What is included in Urban Shield™ and is there an extra cost?
Urban Shield™ is an advanced, hospital-grade antimicrobial sanitising barrier that eliminates 99.9% of bacteria, germs, and viral pathogens. It is included 100% free of charge with every carpet and upholstery booking.
Is your timber floor sanding truly dust-free?
Yes. We connect heavy-duty multi-stage HEPA containment extractors directly to the cutting head of our drum, belt, and edge sanders. This captures 99% of airborne timber dust at the source.
How does your 14-day satisfaction guarantee work?
If you notice any missed spot, stain re-emergence, or have any quality concern within 14 days of our visit, simply call or email us. A senior technician will return to your property and re-treat the area free of charge.
